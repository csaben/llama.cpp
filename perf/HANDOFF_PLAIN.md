- GPU0 baseline completed at 206.77 / 192.09 / 177.93 / 165.01 tok/s (depth 0 / 4096 / 16384 / 32768). Raw table and 119 complete steady-state token intervals are in perf/p6p. The separate 1 GiB read probe reached 889.3 GB/s on PCI 01:00.0. Q2_0 down is especially inefficient (6.0 MB in 12 us); attention takes about 12 us per visit. First experiments: one warp per expert row and L2 prefetch concurrent with attention.
- Baseline PPL on GPU0 is 3.0530 / 4.2036 with Q8_0 K/V. The GPU0 trace confirmed that a warp processing two Q2_0 output rows reduced the 44 down calls from 12.02 to 10.45 us. Fixed 24:8 attention, four conv taps and 16 relative features reduced 48 attention calls from 12.16 to 10.84 us. Both remain opt-in during testing. Q2_K warp tiles, loading 0.5-4 MiB of output weights alongside attention, one split, router-conv, fused controller norm and the existing persistent projection did not improve on those two candidates. Raw screening runs and trace summaries are in perf/p6p.
- A two-limb INT8 tensor-core QK experiment and a fused down/conv experiment are undergoing evaluation. Live model accounting: dense IQ4_NL matrices excluding the head are 726.76 MB/pass through the 48 visits; routed weights 1019.22 MB/token; head 260.53 MB. Controller weights on the two returning passes are 113.25 MB at F16; router 56.62 MB. The brief's 1135 MB dense figure includes more than the actual IQ4_NL matrices.
- Two-row Q2_0 reproduced all 64 full-vocabulary logit rows after a 200-token prefix bit for bit. Shape specialization of attention changes rounded logits, so it needs the quality gate. Combined four-depth benchmark was 210.03 / 197.51 / 184.33 / 170.90. The INT8 QK experiment passed decode PPL (4.2094) but was slower at all four depths. A corrected graph-level weighted-sum/conv fusion reached 203.62-206.08 at depth 0, compared with 204.77-206.01 controls; the earlier graph matcher did not fire. Keeping only the two-row stand-alone expert path.
- Published the working results and source attributions in perf/RESULTS_PLAIN.md. A dedicated current-KV CTA helps at the shortest depth but hurts the deeper scan; use it only for the smallest cache if the final PPL passes. Its unrestricted decode PPL was 4.1984. The two-stage router warp sort is under test. A CUDA cooperative grid really replaced all three Q2 expert operations in 44 of the 48 visits; the first version took 34.99 us/visit. Testing a second version with independent rows unrolled and slot-local readiness.
- The cooperative expert grid reproduced the 64 reference logit rows, but its 34.99 us/visit exceeded the original three launches' 33.22 us. Local expert barriers, unrolled rows and prefetch did not close the gap. A diagnostic replacing quantized matvecs by streamed reads and zero outputs reached 215.26 tok/s; deleting those reads as well reached 450.69. This tests the cost of the measured graph, with synthetic activations. No diagnostic runs count as model results.
- The 128-to-8 router warp sort gained about 1 tok/s in two paired trials. Splitting out the single-state router itself did not help. The 256-cell attention schedule (one block per KV head, skip fully masked 16-cell tiles) gained 4.4 tok/s in a five-repeat ABBA trial. At larger caches the existing ten-split limit beat 5, 20 and 32. A synthetic lossless dense layout with evict-first hints improved one input projection by 0.35 us but slowed the head; keeping the GGUF layout. Preparing the final build, four-depth benchmarks and quality checks.
