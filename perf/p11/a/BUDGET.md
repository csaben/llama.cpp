# Stream A exchange budget

At 241 tokens/s the serial budget is 4,149 us/token. The [prior depth-zero profile](../../p8/base/latency-mean.md) counts 48 attention visits and 48 expert visits (24 physical blocks reused), per token. A hypothetical tensor split with two collective boundaries per visit-pair has 96 boundaries. At the brief's 2.9 us per 9 KiB write the optimistic cost is 278 us/token for a one-way exchange at each boundary, or 557 us for serial send and return. Flags, waits, reductions, larger payloads, launch gaps and load imbalance are extra. An ideal halving of the entire serial time saves at most 2,075 us before communication. This is a ceiling, not a prediction.

Expert gate/up and down alone used 817 + 416 us for the 44 Q2 visits and 120 + 63 us for four IQ4 visits (1,416 us total). Perfectly halving just those kernels saves at most 708 us. At one send and one return per expert visit (278 us total), at most 430 us remains before other costs. Keeping SITU and down-shards beside gate/up could avoid returning their large intermediate. The output head alone used 298 us. Its row-split ideal is 149 us saved, less the cost of sending the activation and returning about 400 KB of FP32 logits for half of the vocabulary (about 31 us just for payload at 13 GB/s). Stage the fastest configuration only after output checks and paired timings.

The p9 cached DSpark round needs the draft IDs before target verification, and verification determines the committed feature rows for the next round. Placing the drafter on a second card does not itself remove either dependency. Screen placement separately from algorithmic overlap.
