The history of the bicycle begins in the early nineteenth century, when the first wheeled vehicles were created. These early bicycles were simple, two-wheeled contraptions that were propelled by the rider’s feet. They were known as “draisines” or “velocipedes” and were popular among the wealthy and leisure class.

In the late 1800s, the bicycle began to evolve into the modern form we know today. The first safety bicycle was created in 1885 by John Kemp Starley, who added a chain drive and a pneumatic tire to the design. This made the bicycle more efficient and comfortable to ride, and it quickly became popular among the general public.

The bicycle has since become an essential mode of transportation and recreation, and it has played a significant role in shaping our society. In this article, we will explore the history of the bicycle, from its early beginnings to its modern-day applications.
