Explain step by step how TCP slow start and congestion avoidance work.

Answer from AI

TCP slow start and congestion avoidance are mechanisms to control the amount of data sent to avoid network overload.

Slow start:

  • At the beginning of the connection, the sender sends a small amount of data (usually one segment).
  • With each successful acknowledgment, the amount of data sent increases exponentially (doubles).
  • This allows the network to be gradually loaded and detect congestion.

Congestion avoidance:

  • When the sender reaches a certain threshold (ssthresh), the growth of the amount of data sent becomes linear instead of exponential.
  • This helps avoid sudden overload of the network.

If a packet loss is detected, the sender reduces the amount of data sent and restarts the slow start.
