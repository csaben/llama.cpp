The history of the bicycle begins in the early nineteenth century, when the first wheeled vehicles were created. These early bicycles were simple, two-wheeled machines that were propelled by the rider’s feet. They were known as “draisines” or “velocipedes” and were popular among the wealthy and the aristocracy.

In the late 1800s, the bicycle began to gain popularity among the general public. This was due in part to the invention of the safety bicycle, which featured a chain drive and a pneumatic tire. The safety bicycle was much more comfortable and easier to ride than the earlier models, and it quickly became the most popular type of bicycle.
