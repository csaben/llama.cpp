Explain step by step how TCP slow start and congestion avoidance work.

Answer from AI

Slow Start:

  • When establishing a connection, the sender has an initial small value of the congestion window (cwnd).
  • Each received ACK increases cwnd exponentially until reaching the threshold (ssthresh).
  • This allows quickly filling the bandwidth without overloading the network.

Congestion Avoidance:

  • When cwnd exceeds ssthresh, increase occurs more linearly (by one MSS per RTT).
  • This helps avoid sharp drops in throughput and prevent packet loss.

When detecting packet loss (e.g., retransmission or timeout), TCP decreases cwnd and ssthresh, shifting to slow start or a more conservative mode.

Example: initially cwnd is small, grows exponentially, then slows down by one packet per RTT until loss occurs, after which cwnd is reset to half or an initial value.
