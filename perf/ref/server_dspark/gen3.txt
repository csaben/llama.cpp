Explain step by step how TCP slow start and congestion avoidance work.

Put simply, it’s all about (read it as “how cold is AWS on this thread” etc):

1) Initial congestion avoidance - is in place so that increasing traffic over the course of an encounter doesn’t kill stuff in some weird mess, right from the get go. Typically there is a lowish constant window of acknowledges that gets progressed by increasing one throughput on a known successful frame or frame for each known transmission. Instead of the framing dependant TCP or similar, you can use random numbers for the frame pointers.

2) Congestion avoidance which is the one that doubles up an ack ratio (slow start) from 2 to 4ish etc and only then passes up to a utilization which can indicate the heat is getting high (say incoming or outflow data volume etc)
