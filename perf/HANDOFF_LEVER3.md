Separating the KQ scores and gated value scan also slowed real decode. Both variants matched 64 full-vocabulary rows after 200 tokens and eight after 16K. The warp moment reduction matched the short reference and cut about 27 us/token across the router, controller down and residual mix. Controller apply and the attention tail are under test. All 7,077,888 router weights round-trip through BF16 exactly; the smaller representation did not improve whole-token throughput. Tile-packed attention gates saved 0.15-0.18 us/call at the cost of a second weight layout. Short-scan history stripes are under test.
