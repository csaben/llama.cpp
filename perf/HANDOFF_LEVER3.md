Finished on branch `inkling-3090-lever3`. The selected source is `2a132fc`, tagged `p7l3-exact-fold-219tok`. The final report and validation are tagged `p7l3-final-219tok`. [Results, replay flags and reproduction](RESULTS_LEVER3.md)

Plain tg128 reached **219.23, 200.86, 187.12 and 173.51 tok/s** at depths 0, 4096, 16384 and 32768. The fresh 16264c6-equivalent baseline measured 217.75, 198.50, 185.95 and 170.84. A same-binary off/on/on/off comparison gained 1.27%, 1.52%, 2.05% and 1.73%. The fold runs by default on the matching GA10x decode shape. Set `IK_PROJ_CONV=0` for the control. `IK_MR=1` uses the established DSpark path. [Baseline](p7l3/baseline.txt), [selected build](p7l3/final.txt), [paired results](RESULTS_LEVER3.md#plain-single-token-decode)

The output projection applies the four-tap short convolution, scale, residual and state writes in its epilogue. CUDA's allocation dependency keeps the Q8_1 input live. All 48 visits fire, at 6.788 us each vs 8.614 us for the original pair. All 64 full-vocabulary rows matched the baseline. Prefill/decode PPL were 3.0530/4.1979 on both builds. All 2834 DSpark comparison rows matched; all 3988 supported CUDA operator cases passed. [Selected trace](p7l3/accepted-trace.txt), [validation](RESULTS_LEVER3.md#correctness)

Nine glue gaps, evict-last hints and three inline prefetch sites incurred more overhead than they hid. Resident expert-down and attention-projection pipelines lost to their original pairs by 6.80 and 4.34 us per staged visit. A replay that gave all 193 real matvecs their inputs and routes up front still took 4.09 ms with glue on an independent graph branch. The 300 tok/s target allows 3.33 ms.
