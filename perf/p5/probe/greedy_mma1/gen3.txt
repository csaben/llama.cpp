Explain step by step how TCP slow start and congestion avoidance work.

TCP slow start and congestion avoidance are two mechanisms used by TCP to manage the flow of data and prevent network congestion. Here's a step-by-step explanation of how they work:

1. Slow Start:
- When a TCP connection is established, the sender starts with a small amount of data in flight, known as the initial congestion window (cwnd).
- The sender sends a small number of packets, and as each packet is acknowledged, the cwnd is increased exponentially.
- This allows the sender to gradually increase the amount of data in flight, avoiding overwhelming the network with a large burst of data.

2. Congestion Avoidance:
- As the sender increases the cwnd, it enters the congestion avoidance phase.
- In this phase, the sender increases the cwnd linearly, rather than exponentially, to avoid congestion.
